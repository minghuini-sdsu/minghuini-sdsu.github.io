Hi, my name is Minghui

I am an Assistant Professor of Psychology at the School of Psychology, Sociology, and Rural Studies, South Dakota State University.

Prior to this position, I worked as a Postdoctoral Associate at Rutgers University-Newark, working with Dr. Luis Rivera in the Rutgers Implicit Social Cognition (RISC) lab.

I obtained my Ph.D. in Social Psychology with a minor in Cognitive Science from the Department of Psychology, Cornell University, working primarily with Dr. Vivian Zayas in the Person and Context (PAC) lab.

My research examines the structure and content of our social cognitions and how they influence individual judgment and decision making, reflecting and shaping systemic inequality in diverse contexts, such as workplace, higher education, and healthcare.
